Why I Started Mentor Baooji

I never planned to become a mentor.

My journey started in 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic. Like millions of people around the world, I was searching for a way to build an online income and create a better future for myself and my family. I started with Facebook — and the beginning was extremely difficult. I had no mentor, no roadmap, no one to tell me what worked and what didn't.

My Facebook pages were suspended multiple times. Months of hard work disappeared overnight. Every time I lost a page, I had only two choices: quit, or start again from zero. I chose to start again — not once, but every time it happened.

Instead of giving up, I kept experimenting. I learned through failure, tested everything myself, and let every mistake teach me something new. Slowly I stopped following random advice and started understanding how Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube actually work — through real experience, not theory.

In 2022, I received my very first social media payment: one hundred and sixty dollars. It wasn't a huge amount of money, but it completely changed my mindset. That payment proved one thing: online income is real for people who are willing to learn real skills and stay consistent.

After that, I worked harder. I expanded into TikTok, explored YouTube, adopted AI tools as they emerged, and kept building practical systems that produce real results instead of empty promises.

In December 2022, I started mentoring others. Today, I've trained more than 900 students — many of them now earning through Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, AI tools, and other digital skills.

One thing inspires me every single day: the diversity of the people I teach. My youngest successful student started at just 15 years old. My oldest learner was 67.

Age has never been the barrier. The willingness to learn has always been the difference.

I regularly share my students' success stories and payment proofs inside my WhatsApp Channel — not to impress people, but to show that real guidance creates real results.

Mentor Baooji exists because I don't want people to spend years making the same mistakes I made. The internet doesn't suffer from a lack of information. It suffers from a lack of clarity. People don't need another hundred tutorials — they need practical guidance, honest mentorship, and someone who has already walked the road they're about to begin.

Technology will keep changing. Platforms will keep evolving. AI will become even more powerful. But one thing will never change: people who invest in real skills will always have opportunities. That is what I teach. That is what Mentor Baooji stands for.

My mission isn't to create followers. My mission is to create independent earners who can build a future with real digital skills.

Baooji Founder, Mentor Baooji

Baooji Says

Consistency will always outperform intensity.

I've watched hundreds of students start strong and quit before the results ever arrived. The urge to work with intensity for a week and then disappear for a month is one of the most expensive habits in this line of work.

What actually compounds is far less exciting: showing up on the days you don't feel like it, publishing when you're unsure, staying in the game long after the initial motivation runs out. The platforms change. The algorithms change. This does not.

If you take one lesson from me and forget everything else, take this one. Consistency isn't a personality trait — it's a decision you make again on the days it feels pointless.
