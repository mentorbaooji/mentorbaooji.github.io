Why "Mentor Baooji"

The name behind the name

Mentor, because I mentor people — it has become my life's work.

Baooji comes from my late father, Riaz Ul Haq — it was his family nickname.

Students gradually began calling me Mentor Baooji. Over time, it became my identity.

Who I Mentor

I mentor people who genuinely want to change their lives — not people looking for shortcuts.

My Values

What guides every student relationship

Honest Guidance

Straight answers over comfortable ones. If something won't work for you, I'll tell you before you spend time on it.

Real Skills

Practical, working knowledge you can apply immediately — not theory that sounds good but goes nowhere.

Consistency

Small, steady effort repeated over time is what actually builds results — not short bursts of motivation.

Community

Integrity

Never Compromise
