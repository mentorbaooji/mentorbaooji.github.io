Questions

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the Group Learning program?

30 days of structured learning with 5 recorded classes every week, AI included throughout, practical assignments, and WhatsApp Support in a batch of 30–40 students.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Every program is built to take you from beginner to advanced — no prior experience is required to start.

Can I learn using only a mobile phone?

Yes. Many students learn and manage their pages entirely from a mobile phone.

What if I can't attend a live class?

Group Learning classes are recorded, so you can go through them at a time that works for you.

How is AI included in the courses?

Prompt engineering and practical AI tools are woven into every program — including AI images, stories, scripts and research, using tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Kimi.

What payment methods are accepted?

JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer and Binance.

When does the next batch start?

New batches open every second month. Message the WhatsApp Channel for the next confirmed date.
