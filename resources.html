Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are these resources free?

Yes. Every resource in this library is completely free, with no hidden cost or paid upgrade required.

Will more resources be added?

Yes. This library will keep growing as platforms evolve and as new lessons prove useful to real students.

Can beginners use these?

Yes — every resource here is written for people starting from zero, not for people who already have experience.

Can I learn using only a mobile phone?

Yes. Many students learn and manage their pages entirely from a mobile phone, so these resources are built with that in mind.

Do I need English?

No. These resources are written to be simple and practical — the skills themselves don't require advanced English. Clarity matters more than fluency.

How often are resources updated?

Whenever a platform changes in a way that affects what actually works. Updates happen when they're genuinely useful, not on a fixed schedule.

Want everything in one place?

Every resource on this page, plus new ones as they're released, is shared first inside the WhatsApp Community.
